## Full description (the long sales page body)

Most gig drivers leave $3,000–$10,000 on the table every tax year. Not because the IRS is greedy — because they didn't track miles, or they tried to "guess" at the end of the year, or they trusted the in-app mileage estimate (which only counts active deliveries and misses 30–50% of your real deductible miles).

This is the tracker that fixes that.

**What you get**

- A pre-built Excel spreadsheet that opens in Excel, Numbers, OR Google Sheets — drop it in Google Drive and it just works
- A daily Trip Log with dropdowns for your vehicles and platforms — 10 seconds per trip
- Auto-calculated business miles and dollar deduction using the IRS standard mileage rate (editable for any tax year)
- A Monthly Summary tab that auto-aggregates your year — total miles, deductions, and a per-platform breakdown
- A Year-End Summary that tells you exactly what to put on **Schedule C, Line 9 (Car & Truck Expenses)** when you file
- An IRS Reference tab with plain-English answers to the 10 questions every gig driver asks (standard mileage vs actual expenses, what counts as a business mile, what to do if you forgot to log a day, etc.)
- A built-in audit "reasonableness check" — flags if your business-use percentage is high enough to attract IRS attention

**Built for:**

- DoorDash Dashers
- Uber and Lyft drivers
- Uber Eats, Grubhub, Instacart shoppers
- Amazon Flex drivers
- TaskRabbit Taskers
- Rover sitters and walkers
- Shipt, Roadie, and anyone else 1099 on wheels

If you drive for more than one platform, this tracker handles all of them in one file. The Monthly Summary breaks down miles and deductions by platform automatically.

**Why this beats the apps**

Stride, MileIQ, Everlance, and the rest are great until they aren't. They cost $5–$10 per month forever, eat your phone battery, occasionally miss trips, and lock your data inside their cloud. This is a one-time $9, your data lives in your own Drive or hard drive, and you can audit and edit every formula yourself.

**What's actually in the file (6 tabs)**

1. **Start Here** — 3-step onboarding and IRS requirements explained in plain English
2. **Setup** — Pick your tax year, set the IRS mileage rate, add your vehicles and platforms (everything else reads from here)
3. **Trip Log** — One row per trip. Dropdowns for vehicle and platform. Enter miles directly OR enter start/end odometer — whichever is faster. Default is 100% business so most trips are one click.
4. **Monthly Summary** — Auto-aggregated by month, with a separate per-platform breakdown
5. **Year-End Summary** — Total deduction, Schedule C line 9 amount, reasonableness check, records to keep
6. **IRS Reference** — Plain-English answers to standard mileage vs actual, commute rules, audit lookback, and 7 more

**No subscription. No app permissions. No data leaving your computer.**

Delivered as a single .xlsx file. Opens in Excel 2016+, Excel for Mac, Numbers (with light formatting changes), or Google Sheets (just drag into Drive — Sheets converts it cleanly and all the formulas work).

---

**A note on the IRS rate:** The 2025 rate was $0.70/mile. The 2026 rate is editable on the Setup tab — verify it at irs.gov/standardmileagerates each January. We update the default annually.

**Refunds:** 7-day no-questions-back, like every Gumroad product. If you don't use it, get your money back.

**Not tax advice.** Educational tool. For complex situations (employer reimbursements, multiple businesses, leased vehicles), consult a CPA.
