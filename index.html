Common questions

Still not sure? Here are the things people ask before checking their hustle.

Is this actual legal advice?
+
No — and that's intentional. SideHustleGuard provides general educational information, not legal or tax advice. Think of it as a knowledgeable friend explaining your situation in plain English. For complex situations, always consult a licensed CPA or attorney.
I sell on Etsy — do I really need this?
+
Yes. Etsy sellers are self-employed the moment they make their first sale. That means self-employment tax, potential quarterly payments, and state-specific rules most sellers don't know about until they get a surprise bill. A 60-second check can save you from a very bad tax season.
What if I haven't made any money yet?
+
That's actually the best time to use this. Setting up correctly from day one is much easier than fixing problems later. We'll tell you exactly what to set up now so you're protected before the money starts coming in.
What exactly is in the $5 report?
+
A full breakdown of every issue found, a prioritized action plan, a personalized tax deadline calendar, a deduction checklist specific to your hustle type, and a PDF you can download and share with your CPA. Everything is tailored to your state and income level.
Do you store my information?
+
No. We don't store any personally identifiable information. Your answers are used only to generate your report and are not saved, sold, or shared with anyone.
